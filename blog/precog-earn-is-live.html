PreCog Earn is live

The desktop node is out: real RandomX mining, an auditable share ledger, and a declared economics blueprint that pays users for the work they do — released pro-rata after human review. It is small, it is honest, and it is shipped.

By PreCog Security · November 2026

What shipped, not what I wanted to ship

The PreCog Earn desktop node (v0.1.0) is a consent-gated wrapper around XMRig v6.26.0, pinned and hash-verified against the upstream SHA256SUMS before anything executes on your machine. It mines RandomX to the operator-configured Monero address. It has no tray ghost, no autostart, no background persistence: close the window and the miner dies with it.

Every valid share your node submits lands on a hash-chained, append-only ledger. Balances are recomputed from the chain — there are no mutable counters to manipulate, no quiet over-crediting, and any retroactive edit breaks the chain for everything after it. Duplicate receipts are rejected and logged.

The economics, in public

The treasury receives all mined coin. Your credits accrue on the ledger. At each settlement, half of declared net proceeds funds the redemption pool; your payout equals pool × credits_u / Σcredits. Drafts are generated per handle. A human maps handles to declared destinations, reviews every payout, and approves every send. There is no automatic settlement: the ledger stores drafts, humans sign releases.

Honest numbers

  • Node v0.1.0 — Windows, macOS, Linux; requires Python 3.9+; no admin rights.
  • Reference measurement: ~170 H/s per 3 threads on a 2012 i7-class CPU. That is the order of magnitude, not a promise.
  • Payout cadence: per redemption cycle, after threshold. No interpolation, no weighting, no loyalty tiers.
  • Credits are redemption units, not a security. Not an investment. No promise of return.

What comes next, still public

GPU kernels, Android distribution after store review, cohort payout dry-runs before scaling, and a published post-mortem of the first settlement. If any of that disappoints, the ledger will show it before we say anything in prose.

Install

Download the node v0.1.2 (SHA256SUMS). Double-click earn.bat, tick consent, press Start mining. Or, from a terminal:

python precog-earn-node.py --i-consent --start
