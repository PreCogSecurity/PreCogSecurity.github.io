The Cost of Ignoring Security

In 2025 alone, security breaches cost companies an average of $4.88 million per incident. For startups and small businesses, a single vulnerability can mean the end.

The problem? Most teams only discover security issues after they've been exploited. Manual code reviews miss things. Traditional scanners are slow and produce false positives.

Enter AI-Powered Scanning

  • Prioritize findings by actual risk
  • Suggest fixes, not just problems
