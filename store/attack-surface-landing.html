Scope & Legal guardrails

This is a legal boundary, not a disclaimer footer. Read it before booking.

Authorization required — always

  • Written authorization & domain ownership proof (DNS TXT, registrar confirmation, or org domain control) are required before any collection begins, and are recorded as an ops-scope entry.
  • Authorized scope only. We scan only assets you own or control. Assets discovered outside scope become findings; the work stops there — no third-party pivot.
  • No active scanning without authorization. Recon is passive/authorized by default. Any active, intrusive, or authenticated testing requires a separately scoped and signed engagement.
  • No exploitation, ever. We never exploit a vulnerability, authenticate with found credentials, or access live data. Found secrets are reported as redacted previews; we never store, transmit, or use live secret values.
  • Coordinated disclosure. If a finding exposes third-party data (e.g., a partner's assets or another customer's data), we coordinate responsible disclosure per our MSA rather than silently reporting it to you.
  • Lawful only. All work stays inside the applicable legal framework (including applicable anti-hacking statutes). Asset discovery for unscoped targets is reported as out-of-scope, not pursued.
  • Advisory only. A recon report is not a penetration test, not a certified assessment, and not legal advice. Coverage depends on public/authorized sources; we do not guarantee completeness or absence of unknown assets.

Questions buyers ask

Is this a penetration test?

No. This is passive, authorized reconnaissance and asset inventory. We identify and prioritize exposure; we do not exploit vulnerabilities, attempt intrusion, or alter your systems. Active testing requires a separately scoped engagement.

What do you need from me to start?

Written authorization naming the in-scope domains, proof you own or control them, and an agreed asset list. We record this as an ops-scope entry before any data collection. Typical onboarding completes in one business day.

Do you store secrets you find?

No. Found secrets are reported as redacted previews with enough context to locate and rotate them (file path, line range, commit hash). We never store, transmit, or use live secret values; full engagement runs under NDA and MSA with a liability cap.

What if a secret or asset belongs to someone else?

We stop. Anything outside your scoped estate is reported as out-of-scope. If a finding exposes third-party data, we coordinate responsible disclosure per the MSA rather than pursue it.

How complete can a one-off report be?

Completeness depends on public and authorized sources plus your confirmed asset list. We document scope, sources, timestamps, and limits in the methodology appendix. We do not promise zero unknown assets or guarantee absence of future exposure.

Can I use this for compliance or insurance?

Yes as evidence of a dated, scoped attack-surface review and remediation tracking. It is not a certification or audit opinion; share it alongside your control evidence. Have counsel review how you represent it.
