Strategy A: artefact first

What it is (and isn't)

An external attack-surface report is a dated, scoped inventory of everything the internet can see tied to your brand/domain, with prioritized fixes. It is not a penetration test, certification, or audit opinion. PreCog identifies and prioritizes exposure; we never exploit, authenticate with found secrets, or alter your systems.

Passive recon methodology (no exploitation)

  1. Scope & authorization — signed authorization, domain ownership proof, ops-scope entry, agreed asset list. No collection before this.
  2. DNS & zone evidence — A/AAAA/CNAME/MX/TXT, subdomain candidate lists, wildcard detection.
  3. Certificate Transparency (CT) logs — SANs, forgotten/preview hosts, issuance history. Highest-yield, lowest-risk source.
  4. Public search operators — indexed references to domain/brand in repos, gists, pastes, docs.
  5. Service fingerprinting (passive) — HTTP headers/banners on known paths, TLS handshake only. No login attempts.
  6. TLS & certificate hygiene — version/cipher, chain, expiry, SAN, HSTS, redirect correctness.
  7. Correlation & attribution — dedupe assets, attribute to your estate, separate in-scope vs out-of-scope.
  8. Secret reference triage — locate lept leaks (paths/locators) but never store or use live values.
  9. Prioritized reporting — P1/P2/P3 with owner hints, effort, verification steps + methodology appendix.

Do / don't (scope discipline)

  • DO: get written authorization + ownership proof first.
  • DO: enumerate via passive sources (DNS, CT, search) first.
  • DO: treat any unknown host as a finding, not a target.
  • DO: report secret locators/previews only — never values.
  • DO: stop at the scope boundary; report, don't pursue.
  • DON'T: brute-force subdomains or directories without authorization.
  • DON'T: attempt login, auth, or use of found creds.
  • DON'T: pivot into third-party assets or tenants.
  • DON'T: store/transmit/use live secret values.
  • DON'T: claim "certified secure" — it's a dated, scoped review.

Scope & Legal guardrails

Authorization required — always. No collection without written authorization + domain ownership proof, recorded as an ops-scope entry. Out-of-scope assets become findings, not targets — no third-party pivot. No active scanning/exploitation without a separately scoped engagement.
  • Lawful only (incl. applicable anti-hacking statutes).
  • Coordinated disclosure if third-party data is encountered.
  • Never store/transmit/live-use of exposed credentials.
  • Advisory only — not legal advice, pen test, or audit.

Common "forgotten asset" classes

  • CT-log hosts with no DNS record (e.g., admin-staging).
  • Dormant preview-N build hosts.
  • Dev sandboxes live without auth.
  • Abandoned promo/lander pages still ingesting PII.
  • Legacy WP/blog estates.
  • Unmanaged metrics/Grafana endpoints.
  • Expired certs breaking SDKs/SSO.
