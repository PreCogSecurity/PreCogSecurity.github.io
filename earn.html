How payouts work

The pool pays you.
Not a credits scheme.

Mining pools are a solved, decade-old technology. A real pool verifies every share against the chain, tracks your contribution, and pays your wallet directly. We plug into one — we do not ask you to mine for internal credits.

Step 1 — Your node mines

Hash-verified XMRig, your rules

node --wallet <YOUR_XMR> --i-consent --start

A pinned, SHA-256-verified XMRig does RandomX work on threads you choose. It needs no admin rights and stops the moment you stop it.

Step 2 — The pool verifies

Real share verification

pool verifies hash < target

The pool checks every submitted share against the live network target. Accepted shares are recorded in the pool's own accounting — battle-tested, not a bespoke ledger we invented.

Step 3 — Honest pool economics

You keep the pool's payout

payout = shares / pool_total × block_reward − fee

Pools publish their fee and payout threshold. Your earnings are the pool's own accounting, paid in XMR to your address — no 50/50 split with us, no declaration we have to make.

Step 4 — No custody, no wait

Direct to your wallet

pool → YOUR_XMR_ADDRESS

PreCog never holds your coins or your keys. When you cross the pool's threshold, it pays your wallet directly. Stop any time; nothing is locked.

We are honest about what we are: a thin, consent-first wrapper around real mining infrastructure. If you want to run your own pool, use mature pool software — that is not something to rebuild here.

How it works

From download
to paid shares.

Four steps, zero ambiguity. If any step is unclear, email us — we answer as the humans who wrote it.

  1. 01

    Download

    precog-earn-desktop-v0.1.0.zip (SHA256SUMS). x64 Windows/macOS/Linux. Requires Python 3.9+. No admin rights, no kernel drivers, no AV exceptions.

  2. 02

    Consent

    Run earn.bat (double-click) or python precog-earn-node.py --i-consent --start. The node downloads the pinned XMRig v6.26.0 and verifies its SHA-256 before anything executes. Ctrl-C or window close stops everything.

  3. 03

    Mine

    RandomX (Monero) proof-of-work on your consented threads, submitted to a real public pool that verifies every share.

  4. 04

    Get paid

    The pool pays your own XMR wallet directly at its threshold. No PreCog credits, no custody, no settlement scheme — you keep what the pool credits you, minus its published fee.

Straight answers

The honest FAQ.

Is this mining real?

Yes. The desktop node mines RandomX to a public Monero pool. Your shares settle against real blocks; the ledger balances against them.

Who has my keys?

Nobody. The node never sees your keys, your passwords, or your files. It talks to one TLS endpoint and writes a plain-text ledger in its own folder.

Can payouts be faked?

No. Real proof of work is verified by the pool against the chain, and payments come from the pool's own transparent accounting to your own address. PreCog cannot fabricate a pool payout.

What does it cost me?

Electricity, plus the pool's published fee. You are free to stop at any time. PreCog takes no cut of your mining rewards.

Is this an investment?

No. It is paid work: you contribute hashpower, the pool pays in XMR. There is no token, no promised return, no lock-up.

How do I opt out?

Close the window, Ctrl-C, or uninstall. Mining stops immediately; any shares the pool already accepted are settled by the pool on its normal schedule.
