50/50 economics

You keep half.
By public accounting.

Every signing off the books changes somebody's credits. That is why the books are public by construction. These are the mechanics, in full.

Step 1 — Treasury mines

The company pools hashpower

treasury XMR += node_share(job)

Your operator-configured node connects to a public pool; block proceeds settle to the operator-blessed network rail. Nothing of yours is pooled differently than the company's — same pool, same shares.

Step 2 — You accrue credits

Valid shares, hash-chained

ledger += {ts, user, job, shares, hash(prev)}

Every valid receipt is appended to a publishable, append-only ledger. Duplicate receipts are rejected and logged. Balances are recomputed from the chain — there are no mutable counters to fiddle with.

Step 3 — Redemption pool

50% of declared net proceeds

pool = 0.5 × (gross − pool_fee − net_fee − wear)

At each sign-off, the operator declares net proceeds. Half is earmarked for redemption; half funds the service, fees, and treasury. Contested math? The ledger disagrees with you — publicly.

Step 4 — Payout at threshold

Pro-rata, consensus-governed

payout_u = pool × credits_u / Σcredits

When the outstanding balance crosses threshold, drafts are generated per user. A human maps handle → declared payout destination, reviews, and approves. Automatic settlement executes when proofs, thresholds, and consensus policy are satisfied.

Equal-split mode (1/N) exists as an opt-in cohort with identical audits and anti-free-rider checks; proportional-by-shares is the universal default because it survives unequal devices honestly.

How it works

From download
to paid shares.

Four steps, zero ambiguity. If any step is unclear, email us — we answer as the humans who wrote it.

  1. 01

    Download

    precog-earn-desktop-v0.1.0.zip (SHA256SUMS). x64 Windows/macOS/Linux. Requires Python 3.9+. No admin rights, no kernel drivers, no AV exceptions.

  2. 02

    Consent

    Run earn.bat (double-click) or python precog-earn-node.py --i-consent --start. The node downloads the pinned XMRig v6.26.0 and verifies its SHA-256 before anything executes. Ctrl-C or window close stops everything.

  3. 03

    Mine

    RandomX (Monero) proof-of-work on your consented threads. Your node reports receipts; the company treasury mines; your credits accrue on the public ledger at every valid receipt.

  4. 04

    Automatic settlement

    Credits redeem pro-rata from the declared 50% pool when threshold and consensus rules are satisfied. Payouts route to your declared destination via the settlement rail — deterministic, auditable, and designed without a human approval bottleneck.

Straight answers

The honest FAQ.

Is this mining real?

Yes. The desktop node mines RandomX to a public Monero pool. Your shares settle against real blocks; the ledger balances against them.

Who has my keys?

Nobody. The node never sees your keys, your passwords, or your files. It talks to one TLS endpoint and writes a plain-text ledger in its own folder.

Can payouts be faked?

Not silently. Balances derive from the hash-chained ledger and signed receipts; settlements execute only when deterministic consensus rules are satisfied and every action is logged with proofs, rail, timestamp, and destination.

What does it cost me?

Electricity. That is why you are free to stop at any time and why the 50/50 split compensates the treasury for hosting the pool and the service.

Is this an investment?

No. Credits are redemption units, not a security. There is no promise of return, no price discovery for credits, no lock-up, no transfer market.

How do I opt out?

Close the window (or Ctrl-C, or uninstall the node). Any remaining balance redeems at the next settlement cycle if you meet threshold; otherwise it carries forward free of charge.
