Where PreCog Security wins

The advantages rivals don't have

Six measured advantages, ranked by how often they change a prospect's decision.

  • Verified exploit, not asserted finding You get a working exploit, the patch as a diff, the detection that catches it, and the regression test that proves it stays fixed. Rival scanners report candidates; we ship the fix and the catch.
  • In-repo, clone-and-verify proof The evidence lives in your repository. A prospect can clone, reproduce, and check â€” no PDF claims on trust.
  • A published $500 entry, no sales call From our rate card: Single Repo Audit $500, Enterprise Hardening $2,000, Autonomous Bounty Radar Mesh $1,200/mo. Honesty over hype: no hourly billing, no surprises.
  • Detections we publish, not just findings Sigma/YARA-shaped detection rules land in the repo, so the finding is caught again after the fix â€” rivals ship one-line alerts.
  • A continuous pricing story (honesty over lists) Compared with rivals that go silent on price â€” we publish every tier and explicitly admit where a consultancy is better (compliance attestation, named sign-off).
  • First finding in 48h (free scan) â€” and faster with agents Our free scan gets a working finding in 48h with full evidence. True automated alternatives are slower (Aikedo <3min but different depth, Snyk/Semgrep CI-time).
